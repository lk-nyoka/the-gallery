          How The Gallery works

          A live football production desk. AI agents spot the stories in a match and write them up; plain code checks every fact against the match data; you decide what goes on air.

          Try it in one minute

          1. Press ▶ Play. Events stream in and the chart shows who is attacking.
          2. Stories appear under Top stories now. Click one.
          3. Hover a sentence to see the data behind it, then press Approve for air.
          4. Press Half-time to build the package for the break.

          What each control does

          Match
          Which match you are working on. Load another opens a test fixture, the organisers' data file or a match you log by hand.
          Replay
          Plays the match event by event, as if it were live. Speed sets how fast match time passes.
          Show stories for
          Who the stories are for. Brazil and South Africa get a different lead story and their own language. A single fan lets you pick casual or analyst depth, a favourite club and a player to follow.
          Build package
          Gathers every approved story into what a studio needs at the break: script, graphics data, clip times, recap and a social post, plus the rights terms.
          Test tools
          Plant 2 false sentences makes the AI writer slip a labelled lie into its next two drafts, so you can watch the checker stop it.
          On screen
          What viewers would see: the score bug, approved stories as lower thirds, and automatic name tags when a shot passes 100 km/h or a pass passes 40 m. Copy overlay feed gives the timed data a graphics system would read.
          Readiness
          One score for "can we go to the break?": enough approved stories, every goal and red card covered, no outstanding corrections.
          Who is on top
          Attacking threat per minute for each team, with goals and red cards marked. The sentence under it says what the last ten minutes looked like.

          The marks on a story

          Checked
          Every number was re-computed from the match data and matched.
          Needs your eye
          The numbers are right, but a word like "brilliant" or "wins" is a judgement.
          Blocked
          A wrong number, a claim the data can't support (like "tired"), or no claim at all. Blocked text can never be approved.
          Waiting / Approved
          Waiting means checked and ready for you. Approved means you put it on air.
          Corrected
          The feed changed a fact after you approved. The old story is pulled and a fixed version waits for you.

          The agents

          Editor-in-Chief
          Chooses which detected moments become stories. Goals, red cards and turning points always do.
          Analyst
          Gathers the evidence using read-only tools.
          Writer
          Writes the story from the evidence only, and rewrites it if the checker rejects it.
          Checker
          Not an AI. Code that re-computes every claim. The only part allowed to say what is true.
          Desk, Corrections, Localiser
          Fill gaps in coverage, fix stories when the data changes, and adapt stories for other markets.

          All clubs, players and matches here are fictional and generated for testing.
