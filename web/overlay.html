On-air graphics page

This page draws The Gallery's graphics on a transparent background, so you can lay it over your programme feed in OBS, vMix, Wirecast or a CasparCG HTML template.

  1. Open the console, go to On screen → Use in your graphics system and copy the link. It already contains your match.
  2. In your switcher, add a Browser source (OBS) or Web Browser input (vMix), paste the link and set the size to 1920 × 1080.
  3. Approve stories in the console. Each one appears here as a lower third; player tags for fast shots and long passes appear on their own.

Options you can add to the link: &bug=0 hides the score bug · &bg=green gives a green-screen background · &bg=preview shows a dark background for checking the look.
